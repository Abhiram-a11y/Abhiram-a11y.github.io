Bolted Joint Analysis

Creo for geometry · Ansys Mechanical 2026 R1

Objective

Investigate preload, external-load sharing and structural response in a steel bolted joint, using analytical estimates alongside finite element results.

Model and setup

  • M10 bolt and nut clamping two steel plates, with frictional contact
  • Reported plate dimensions: 60 × 40 × 10 mm each; clearance hole: 11 mm
  • Reported load sequence: 26,100 N pretension in Step 1, locked pretension and 6,000 N external force in Step 2
  • Global mesh element-size setting: 3 mm

Analytical reference

Using the M10 tensile stress area of 58 mm², nominal preload stress is 26,100 / 58 = 450 MPa. A bolt force of 26,300 N corresponds to a nominal tensile-area stress of approximately 453.4 MPa.

The displayed whole-body stress average and local peaks use different scopes from the analytical tensile-area estimate. A section-based axial-force check and mesh study are needed before claiming stress agreement. The tensile stress area is an analytical reference, rather than a confirmed cross-section of the simplified CAD bolt.

Project focus

Two-step pretension modelling, frictional contact, bolt-force extraction and interpretation of nominal stress versus local finite element stress. Input load definitions and fixed-support scoping are reported setup details; the supplied screenshots document the mesh and solution results.

Objective

Investigate the structural response of a perforated brake disc under applied pressure and rotational loading, with the hub face constrained.

  • Pressure: 2 × 10−6 MPa on one annular face
